## What's changed
**Fixed**
- **Overlay Glow Edge Clipping**: fixed rectangular window boundary clipping on peak glow pulsation during recording state by capping peak blur radius to 20px, ensuring smooth fade into transparent margins.
- **Toggle Knob Contrast**: updated toggle switch knobs to use dynamic accent text tokens, ensuring high contrast and visibility across all themes (including White accent color).

**Improved**
- **Accent Color Previews**: replaced single circle previews in Settings -> General with mini overlay capsule mockups displaying recording equalizer bars in the corresponding accent color.
- **One-Way Accent Theme Auto-Switching**: selecting White or Black accent color now automatically sets Dark or Light theme respectively to prevent bad contrast combinations by default.
